# Spare — Support

Spare is a simple calorie counter: you type calories, it shows what's left today. Questions and problems: shalankinkv@gmail.com. You will get a reply from the person who made the app.

## How do I add an entry?

Tap the + button at the bottom of the Today screen, type the calories, tap Add. The name is optional. Amounts you use often appear as chips above the keypad; tap one to add it again.

## How do I delete an entry?

Swipe the entry to the left and tap Delete. On the History tab, open Month view, tap a day, and swipe its entries the same way.

## What does Weekly budget do?

With Weekly budget on, calories you do not use today roll into tomorrow, and calories you overspend are taken from tomorrow. The big number on Today is then your running balance. With it off, every day starts at your daily goal. Change it in Settings.

## Why is the number orange?

You are over today's budget. The number shows how far over. Add less tomorrow, or let Weekly budget balance it out.

## Where are the widgets?

Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap +, search for Spare. The Home Screen widget shows what's left today; the Lock Screen widget shows a ring. Tapping either opens the app.

## How does Apple Health work?

Settings > Write to Apple Health. When it is on, each entry is saved to Apple Health as dietary energy. Spare never reads Health data. If you turned writing off in the Health app, turn it on again under Health > Sharing > Apps > Spare.

## Where is my data? Is it backed up?

Everything is stored on your iPhone and included in your normal device backup. Spare has no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes its data; entries already written to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in Health.

## Can I edit an entry?

Not yet. Delete it and add the right amount.

## Something else?

Write to shalankinkv@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
